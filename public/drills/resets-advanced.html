Baseline resets

Getting to the kitchen line safely means handling hard balls on the way in. A reset takes the pace off the ball and lands it softly in the kitchen so you can keep moving forward.

15 minutestime
A partner, a bucket of ballswhat you need

You've got it when

6 of 10 resets.

Setup

Start at your baseline. Your partner is at the kitchen line on the other side.

How to do it

  1. Your partner drives a firm ball at you.
  2. Reset it into the kitchen with a soft, lifting motion. From the baseline it needs more arc.
  3. Move forward a step or two after each good reset, then reset the next ball from there.
  4. Count the resets that land in the kitchen.

Common mistakes

  • Trying to hit a winner from the baseline. The goal is soft and safe.
  • Backing up to give yourself time. Stand your ground, or step forward.

Make it easier

Start a few steps inside the baseline.

Make it harder

Play the point out after each reset: you must get to the kitchen line and win the dink rally.
