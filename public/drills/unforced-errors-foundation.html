Unforced errors · Foundation drill

Margin game

At most levels, games are lost more than won. Cutting one or two errors a game is often the fastest way to move up.

One gametime
A normal game with your groupwhat you need

You've got it when

1 fewer error per game each week.

Setup

Play a regular game. Before it starts, decide your target: every shot passes 1 to 2 feet over the net and lands at least a foot inside the lines.

How to do it

  1. Play normally, but aim for the safe window instead of the lines.
  2. Count your own unforced errors: balls into the net or out that nobody forced.
  3. Count each one twice in your head. It makes them sting enough to change.
  4. Write down your total after the game. Try to beat it by one next week.

Common mistakes

  • Counting shots your opponents forced. Only count your own free misses.
  • Playing too safe and floating balls. Safe means margin, not slow.

Make it easier

Count only balls into the net for the first week.

Make it harder

Also count balls you popped up that got put away.
