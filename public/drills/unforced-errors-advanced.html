Unforced errors · Advanced drill

Deep margin game

At most levels, games are lost more than won. Cutting one or two errors a game is often the fastest way to move up.

One gametime
A normal game with your groupwhat you need

You've got it when

1 fewer error per game each week.

Setup

Play a regular game. Your serves, returns and drives must land in the back third of the court, but still a foot inside the lines.

How to do it

  1. Aim deep and safe on every serve, return and drive.
  2. Count your unforced errors and every short ball you gave the other team.
  3. Write down both numbers after the game.
  4. Next week, beat at least one of them.

Common mistakes

  • Chasing depth by hitting harder. Get depth from height over the net.
  • Forgetting to count. Keep a tally on your fingers or between points.

Make it easier

Only serves and returns must land deep.

Make it harder

Errors and short balls both count double.
