20 drills for the 10 skills that decide your level

Foundation drills build the basic skill. Advanced drills add choices and pressure. Not sure where to start? Take the Level Check and get the three drills that will help you most.

Serve

A deep serve keeps your opponents back at the baseline, which buys your team time to get to the kitchen line. A short serve hands them the net.

  • Foundation Towel target serves
  • Advanced Deep middle, deep wide

Return

The returning team has the easier path to the net. A deep return plus a quick walk forward puts you at the kitchen line before the third shot.

  • Foundation Return and run
  • Advanced Backhand corner returns

Third shot

The third shot is how the serving team gets to the net. A soft drop that lands in the kitchen lets you move forward safely. A drive that comes back fast leaves you stuck.

  • Foundation Drop ladder
  • Advanced Drop or drive

Dinking

Most points between good players are won or lost in the kitchen. A patient dink rally waits for the other team to pop one up.

  • Foundation Cross-court dink rally
  • Advanced Dink game to 11

Volleys and hand battles

When someone speeds the ball up at you, a calm block wins more points than a big swing. Quiet hands come from repetition.

  • Foundation Wall blocks
  • Advanced Fast hands rounds

Resets and moving forward

Getting to the kitchen line safely means handling hard balls on the way in. A reset takes the pace off the ball and lands it softly in the kitchen so you can keep moving forward.

  • Foundation Mid-court resets
  • Advanced Baseline resets

Backhand

Opponents find your weaker side fast. A steady backhand dink and volley stops it from being the target all game.

  • Foundation Backhand-only dinking
  • Advanced Backhand skinny singles

Unforced errors

At most levels, games are lost more than won. Cutting one or two errors a game is often the fastest way to move up.

  • Foundation Margin game
  • Advanced Deep margin game

Partner play

Doubles is a team game. When partners move together, there are no open gaps in the middle for the other team to hit through.

  • Foundation Rope drill
  • Advanced Stacking rotation

Shot choices

Good players are patient. They attack balls they can hit down and hold back on everything else.

  • Foundation Above the net only
  • Advanced Feet target game
