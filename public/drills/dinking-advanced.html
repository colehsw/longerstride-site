Dink game to 11

Most points between good players are won or lost in the kitchen. A patient dink rally waits for the other team to pop one up.

15 minutestime
A partner (or two pairs)what you need

You've got it when

Win at your level.

Setup

Play points using only the kitchen and the area just behind it. Serve with a dink to start.

How to do it

  1. Dink until someone gives you a ball above net height. Only then may you speed it up.
  2. If you speed up a low ball, you lose the point, even if you win the rally.
  3. Move your partner side to side and to their feet with your dinks to create the high ball.
  4. Play to 11, win by 2.

Common mistakes

  • Attacking out of boredom. The rule is high balls only.
  • Standing still. Take a small step toward each dink so you hit it out in front.

Make it easier

Play to 7 and allow speed-ups only on balls above your shoulders.

Make it harder

Play skinny singles in the kitchen: half the court each, so placement matters more.
