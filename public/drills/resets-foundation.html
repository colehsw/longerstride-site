Mid-court resets

Getting to the kitchen line safely means handling hard balls on the way in. A reset takes the pace off the ball and lands it softly in the kitchen so you can keep moving forward.

15 minutestime
A partner, a bucket of ballswhat you need

You've got it when

6 of 10 reset into the kitchen.

Setup

Stand at mid-court, the "transition zone". Your partner stands at the kitchen line with balls.

How to do it

  1. Your partner hits a firm (not full speed) ball at you.
  2. Hold the paddle out in front with a loose grip, and let the ball hit it. No swing.
  3. Aim to land the ball softly in their kitchen.
  4. After each reset, take a step forward and split-step. Do 10, then switch.

Common mistakes

  • Gripping tight. A loose grip, about a 3 on a scale of 10, soaks up the pace.
  • Swinging back at the ball. Let the paddle absorb it.
  • Moving while the ball is coming. Stop and split-step as they hit.

Make it easier

Have your partner hit at half speed, or feed by hand.

Make it harder

Have your partner mix drives, dinks and lobs so you never know what is coming.
