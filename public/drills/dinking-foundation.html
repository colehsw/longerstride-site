Cross-court dink rally

Most points between good players are won or lost in the kitchen. A patient dink rally waits for the other team to pop one up.

10 minutestime
A partnerwhat you need

You've got it when

20 in a row.

Setup

You and your partner stand at the kitchen line on opposite sides, diagonal from each other.

How to do it

  1. Dink the ball softly cross-court so it lands in your partner's kitchen.
  2. Bend your knees and keep the paddle out in front of you, below the ball.
  3. Push the ball with a short, smooth motion. No backswing.
  4. Count rallies out loud. When you reach 20, switch to the other diagonal.

Common mistakes

  • Standing tall and bending at the waist. Bend your knees so your eyes get closer to the ball.
  • Swinging at the ball. Dinks are a push, not a swing.
  • Aiming too close to the net. Aim for the middle of the kitchen with a little arc.

Make it easier

Dink straight ahead to the partner across from you, which is a shorter, easier shot.

Make it harder

Both of you must land every dink in the back half of the kitchen.
