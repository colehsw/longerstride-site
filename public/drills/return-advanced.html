Backhand corner returns

The returning team has the easier path to the net. A deep return plus a quick walk forward puts you at the kitchen line before the third shot.

15 minutestime
A partner to serve, a towel or conewhat you need

You've got it when

12 of 20 on target.

Setup

Put a towel deep in the server's backhand corner, about 3 feet from the baseline and sideline.

How to do it

  1. Your partner serves. Return toward the towel, deep and to the backhand.
  2. Follow your return to the kitchen line every time.
  3. Count returns that land within a racket length of the towel.
  4. Switch sides of the court halfway so you practice from both.

Common mistakes

  • Going for the corner and missing wide. Aim a foot inside the lines.
  • Forgetting to come in because you are focused on the target.

Make it easier

Make the target the whole back third of the court on the backhand side.

Make it harder

Alternate the backhand corner and the middle, deep, on your partner's call.
