Stacking rotation

Doubles is a team game. When partners move together, there are no open gaps in the middle for the other team to hit through.

20 minutestime
A partner, plus an opposing pair for the gamewhat you need

You've got it when

One full game with no mix-ups.

Setup

Stacking lets each player stay on their best side, for example both forehands in the middle. Agree with your partner who plays left and who plays right.

How to do it

  1. Walk through it first: when your team serves, the server starts on their correct side, and the partner stands next to them on the same side, ready to slide over.
  2. When your team returns, the partner of the returner stands outside the court on the returner's side and moves across after the return.
  3. Practice 10 serves and 10 returns at walking pace until it feels automatic.
  4. Then play a full game stacking every point.

Common mistakes

  • Losing track of the score and the server. Say the score out loud every point.
  • Moving too early and leaving the court open. Switch sides after your shot, not before.

Make it easier

Stack only on your serve for the first game.

Make it harder

Add a hand signal behind your back to "fake" the switch.
