Above the net only

Good players are patient. They attack balls they can hit down and hold back on everything else.

One gametime
A normal game with your groupwhat you need

You've got it when

Fewer popped-up attacks each week.

Setup

Play a regular game with one rule for yourself: you may only hit hard when the ball is above the height of the net when you hit it.

How to do it

  1. Before each shot, ask: is this above the net? If yes, hit down. If no, hit soft.
  2. Count how many times you attacked a low ball and it went into the net or popped up.
  3. Write it down after the game, and try to lower it each week.

Common mistakes

  • Attacking from below the net because the ball is fast. Fast and low still needs a soft shot.
  • Hitting up on a high ball. When it is above the net, hit down.

Make it easier

Play the first game as dinks and drops only, with no attacks, to feel the patience.

Make it harder

Only attack balls above your shoulders.
