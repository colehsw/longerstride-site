Fast hands rounds

When someone speeds the ball up at you, a calm block wins more points than a big swing. Quiet hands come from repetition.

Setup

You and your partner stand at the kitchen line across from each other.

How to do it

  1. Start with soft dinks. Either player may speed up a ball that is above net height.
  2. Volley back and forth without letting the ball bounce. The first missed volley loses the point.
  3. Play as many points as you can in 30 seconds, then rest 30 seconds.
  4. Do 6 rounds and keep score.

Common mistakes

  • Backing up when the ball comes fast. Hold your ground and block.
  • Aiming at the body from too far. Aim at the hip on the paddle side.
  • Swinging big. Short, compact punches only.

Make it easier

Play at three-quarter speed and count any rally of 4 or more volleys as a win.

Make it harder

After you block, reset the ball into the kitchen instead of hitting back hard.
