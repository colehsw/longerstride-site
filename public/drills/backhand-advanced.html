Setup

Use half the court, one side of the center line, for a singles game. Both players use backhands only.

How to do it

  1. Serve and play singles on the half court.
  2. Every shot must be a backhand, including returns and volleys. A forehand loses the point.
  3. Play to 11, win by 2.

Common mistakes

  • Running around the ball to hit a forehand out of habit. Set your feet early for the backhand.
  • Hitting everything hard. Mix drops and dinks with your backhand too.

Make it easier

Allow forehands on serves and returns only.

Make it harder

Your partner may use forehands; you may not.
