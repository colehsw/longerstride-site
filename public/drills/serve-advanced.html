Deep middle, deep wide

A deep serve keeps your opponents back at the baseline, which buys your team time to get to the kitchen line. A short serve hands them the net.

10 minutestime
Two towels, a bucket of balls, half a courtwhat you need

You've got it when

7 of 10 each way.

Setup

Put one towel deep in the box near the center line and one deep near the sideline.

How to do it

  1. Serve to the middle towel, then the wide towel, back and forth.
  2. Keep the same motion for both. Change only where your shoulders and paddle face point.
  3. Do 10 to each towel and score them separately.
  4. Once you can hit both, add a slice or topspin on half your serves.

Common mistakes

  • Giving away the target by turning early. Set up the same way for both serves.
  • Going wide and hitting the sideline. Aim a foot inside it.

Make it easier

Do 10 to one towel before switching, instead of alternating.

Make it harder

Have a partner call "middle" or "wide" just as you start your swing.
