Third shot · Advanced drill

Drop or drive

The third shot is how the serving team gets to the net. A soft drop that lands in the kitchen lets you move forward safely. A drive that comes back fast leaves you stuck.

15 minutestime
A partner to feed from the far baseline, a bucket of ballswhat you need

You've got it when

7 of 10 good choices.

Setup

You are at your baseline. Your partner feeds from their baseline, mixing high bouncing balls and low, short ones.

How to do it

  1. If the ball bounces high and deep enough to hit at waist height or above, drive it low and hard.
  2. If it is low or below your knees, drop it.
  3. Your partner calls each choice good or bad. A low ball driven into the net is a bad choice even if it goes in.
  4. Do 10, then switch. Talk about the ones you disagree on.

Common mistakes

  • Deciding before the ball bounces. Watch the bounce, then choose.
  • Driving every ball because it feels good. Drive only what is above net height at contact.

Make it easier

Have your partner call "high" or "low" as they feed.

Make it harder

Add a third option: drive, then drop the next ball (the "drive and drop") and move forward.
