Feet target game

Good players are patient. They attack balls they can hit down and hold back on everything else.

One gametime
A normal game with your groupwhat you need

You've got it when

More points won at the line.

Setup

Play a regular game. When you attack, aim at an opponent's feet or their paddle-side hip, the hardest places to defend.

How to do it

  1. Wait for a ball above net height, then hit it down at the nearest opponent's feet.
  2. If the ball is at shoulder height, aim at the paddle-side hip instead.
  3. Count points you win at the kitchen line. Compare from week to week.

Common mistakes

  • Aiming at the chest. Players block chest balls easily; feet and hips are harder.
  • Hitting the feet from too far back. Move up to the line first.

Make it easier

Practice it as a drill: a partner feeds high balls and you hit 10 at a towel by their feet.

Make it harder

Follow every feet shot with a split-step, ready for the counter.
