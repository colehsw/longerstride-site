Return and run

The returning team has the easier path to the net. A deep return plus a quick walk forward puts you at the kitchen line before the third shot.

15 minutestime
A partner to serve, a bucket of ballswhat you need

You've got it when

15 of 20 at the line before the third shot.

Setup

Your partner serves from one side. You start in your normal return spot, a step behind the baseline.

How to do it

  1. Return every serve high and deep, toward the middle of their court.
  2. As soon as you hit, walk forward briskly. Do not stop to watch your shot.
  3. Your partner hits the third shot back softly. Count it a success if you are at the kitchen line with your feet set when it arrives.
  4. Do 20 returns, then swap roles.

Common mistakes

  • Admiring the return. The walk forward starts the moment you hit.
  • Running and arriving off balance. Walk fast, then split-step with both feet as their paddle meets the ball.
  • Hitting the return hard and low. A slow, high, deep return gives you more time.

Make it easier

Have your partner serve softly to your forehand.

Make it harder

Have your partner hit a real third shot, drop or drive, and play the point out.
