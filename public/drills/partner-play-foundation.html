Rope drill

Doubles is a team game. When partners move together, there are no open gaps in the middle for the other team to hit through.

10 minutestime
A partner and an opposing pairwhat you need

You've got it when

No gaps in a 10-minute game.

Setup

Before you play, agree on two rules: you stay about 10 feet apart, as if tied by a rope, and the player whose forehand is in the middle takes middle balls.

How to do it

  1. When the ball goes wide to your partner's side, slide over with them so the rope stays tight.
  2. When your partner moves up, you move up. When they go back, you go back.
  3. Call "mine" or "yours" on every middle ball, early.
  4. Play 10 minutes. Afterward, ask each other where the gaps were.

Common mistakes

  • Staying in your half no matter where the ball goes. Shift with the ball.
  • Calling late. Call as soon as the ball leaves the opponent's paddle.

Make it easier

Play the first 5 minutes at dink pace only, so you can focus on moving together.

Make it harder

Ask your opponents to aim every ball at the middle.
