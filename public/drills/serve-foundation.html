Towel target serves

A deep serve keeps your opponents back at the baseline, which buys your team time to get to the kitchen line. A short serve hands them the net.

10 minutestime
A towel, a bucket of balls, half a courtwhat you need

You've got it when

14 of 20 land on the towel.

Setup

Lay a bath towel flat in the back 3 feet of the service box you are serving into. Stand behind your own baseline.

How to do it

  1. Pick one serve motion and stick with it. Same toss or drop, same swing, every time.
  2. Aim high over the net, about 3 to 5 feet. Height is what makes the ball land deep.
  3. Serve 20 balls. Count how many land on the towel or past it but still in.
  4. Between serves, take a breath and reset your feet. Rushing causes most misses.

Common mistakes

  • Hitting harder to get depth. Swing smooth and aim higher instead.
  • Aiming at the line. Aim at the towel so a small miss still lands in.
  • Changing your motion after a miss. Keep it the same and adjust only your aim.

Make it easier

Use a bigger target, like two towels side by side, or stand a step inside the baseline while you find your rhythm.

Make it harder

Fold the towel in half, or count only serves that land on it.
