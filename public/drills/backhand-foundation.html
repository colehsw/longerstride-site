Backhand-only dinking

Opponents find your weaker side fast. A steady backhand dink and volley stops it from being the target all game.

10 minutestime
A partnerwhat you need

You've got it when

20 in a row.

Setup

You and your partner at the kitchen line. Stand so most balls come to your backhand side.

How to do it

  1. Dink with your backhand only. If the ball comes to your forehand, step around and use your backhand anyway.
  2. Lead with the paddle edge and keep your wrist firm.
  3. Push through the ball toward your target, with a short follow-through.
  4. Count the rally. Start again at zero after a miss.

Common mistakes

  • Flipping the wrist. Keep the wrist and forearm moving together.
  • Standing square. Turn your shoulders a little toward the backhand side.

Make it easier

Dink straight ahead instead of cross-court.

Make it harder

Alternate backhand dinks straight and cross-court on every shot.
