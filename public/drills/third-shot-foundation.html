Third shot · Foundation drill

Drop ladder

The third shot is how the serving team gets to the net. A soft drop that lands in the kitchen lets you move forward safely. A drive that comes back fast leaves you stuck.

15 minutestime
A partner, a bucket of ballswhat you need

You've got it when

7 of 10 land in the kitchen before moving back.

Setup

Your partner stands at the kitchen line and feeds you balls. You start at mid-court, halfway between the kitchen and the baseline.

How to do it

  1. Hit a soft shot with a gentle lift, like tossing a ball underhand into a bucket. The ball should peak on your side of the net and fall into their kitchen.
  2. Keep your wrist firm and lift with your shoulder. Finish with the paddle pointing where you want the ball to go.
  3. Hit 10. When 7 land in the kitchen, take two big steps back and repeat.
  4. Keep going until you are hitting from the baseline.

Common mistakes

  • Flicking with the wrist. Lift from the shoulder and keep the wrist quiet.
  • Hitting too low over the net. A drop needs arc, about 2 to 4 feet over the net.
  • Swinging harder as you move back. Make a longer, slower motion instead.

Make it easier

Start just behind the kitchen line and count any ball that lands softly in the kitchen.

Make it harder

After each drop, take one step forward and split-step, as you would in a game.
