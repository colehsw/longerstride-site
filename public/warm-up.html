The 5-Minute Play Forever Warm-Up

Five easy minutes that get your legs, hips and shoulders ready before you play or practice. Do it on the court, in your court shoes, right before your first game.

  1. Walk and swing 1 minute

    Walk the length of the court and back at an easy pace. Swing your arms across your body and back as you go, getting a little bigger each pass.

    Breathe normally. You should be able to chat the whole time.

  2. Side steps 1 minute

    Face the net and side-step along the kitchen line to the sideline and back. Stay a little low with your knees bent, and keep your feet from crossing.

    Small steps are fine. This is the move you use most in a game.

  3. Leg swings and hip circles 1 minute

    Hold the net post or fence. Swing one leg forward and back 10 times, then side to side 10 times. Switch legs. Finish with 5 slow hip circles each way.

    Keep the swings smooth and inside a comfortable range. Never force a stretch.

  4. Shadow swings 1 minute

    Without a ball, make 10 slow forehands, 10 backhands and 5 overhead reaches. Turn your shoulders on each one.

    Start at half speed and build up to your normal swing.

  5. Mini dinks 1 minute

    With a partner, dink back and forth across the kitchen. Soft, easy shots, bending your knees to get low.

    This wakes up your touch before the first point.

If anything hurts, stop. This warm-up is general guidance, not medical advice. If you have a health condition or a recent injury, check with your doctor before starting a new routine.
