Privacy

This page explains what Longer Stride collects when you take the Play Forever Level Check or sign up for the Play Forever Weekly, and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • Your email address, and your first name and ZIP code if you give them.
  • Your Level Check answers, your estimated level and the skills we suggest you work on.
  • Which link or ad brought you here, and basic details your browser sends, such as device type.

How we use it

  • To email your 30-day plan and the Play Forever Weekly, with drills matched to your level.
  • To tell you about socials and clubs near your ZIP code.
  • To see which ads and links bring players to us, so we spend less on the ones that don't.

We don't sell your information. We never show your answers or level to anyone else.

Who helps us

We use Klaviyo to store our email list and send emails, Vercel to run this website, and Meta to measure our Facebook and Instagram ads. Each handles your data only to provide that service.

Your choices

  • Every email has an unsubscribe link. One click and you're off the list.
  • To see, fix or delete what we hold about you, email us at hello@longerstride.com.

Health information

The Level Check asks about your pickleball skills only. It does not ask about your health, and it is not medical advice.
